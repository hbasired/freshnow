02Summary

The answer on one page

  1. Weigh at the van door. A networked scale and a scanner at the loading door. Scan the van once, then each crate once: weight and van are recorded together. No gap between weighing and loading.
  2. Seal, then re-weigh at the machine. A numbered seal on every crate at weighing. The machine loader checks the seal and re-weighs; a short crate raises an alarm.
  3. Cameras as evidence. Number-plate reading at the gate, one camera per loading door, every scan bookmarks the video. Cameras check the record; they don't replace it.
  4. Build the warehouse system into the FreshNow platform, borrowing Manhattan's model (crate IDs, delivery notices, reason codes, cycle counts) and a double-entry weight ledger.
  5. Pilot first: one station, one van, two machines, two weeks. Measure, set the tolerances, then buy the rest.

05The principle

One rule: every kilogram has a from and a to

Each movement of fruit is one ledger row: from a place, to a place, with a weight, a time, a device and a person. Fruit that leaves the business goes to a named virtual place: garbage, charity, staff, sold, cups sold, expected moisture loss.

Received = in stock + on vans + disposed by reason + expected moisture loss + unexplained

"Unexplained" becomes a place you can look at, per handoff, per day, per van. It is the one number this system exists to make visible.

Borrowed from Odoo's double-entry stock moves through virtual locations (inventory adjustment, scrap) and Manhattan's perpetual inventory transactions. believed

07Steps 1–2 · Vendor arrival and receiving

Recording the vendor's vehicle, driver and delivery

A · Guard app

Scan, photograph, done

The guard scans the vendor's delivery QR and photographs the plate and the driver's pass with a phone. Arrival is matched to the expected delivery.

Cost: a phone you already own · Accuracy: high, manual
B · Gate ANPR camera

The camera reads the plate

The camera reads the plate itself and sends it with a photo to the system. The guard only confirms the driver.

Cost: AED 2,040–7,200 Dubai retailers + install AED 500–1,500 one retailer's estimate
C · Weighbridge

Weigh the whole truck

Truck weighed in and out; the difference is the delivery. Only worth it at much larger volumes.

Cost: quote civil works plus equipment

Drivers

Vendors register their drivers; each gets a QR pass. At arrival we keep name, vendor, pass number and a photo. No face recognition: face templates are biometric data, a sensitive category under the UAE data law, and more than this purpose needs believed reading, for counsel.

Receiving

Weigh cartons per pallet on a floor scale and compare with the delivery note. A shortfall above the agreed percentage alerts procurement while the driver is still at the dock.

Hikvision ANPR cameras push plate events to a server over HTTP, as XML — forum reports, varies by model; check the exact model before buying believed.

Recommendation: start with A in the pilot; add B with the warehouse rollout.

08Steps 3–4 · Sorting and unfit fruit

Sorting without silent loss: weigh what goes in and every bin that comes out

Mass balance per sorting batch

Cartons in = good + unfit + bad + unexplained

Each output bin is weighed when it's closed, on the same kind of networked scale as the loading door. Yield is tracked per vendor and per lot, so a vendor's poor fruit shows up as a number.

Unfit fruit leaves with three things: reason, recipient, approver

  • Sold — buyer and price
  • Charity — recipient, photo or signature
  • Staff — within a written allowance (a process limit, not a personal score)
  • Blue bottle project — batch number
  • Machines where looks don't matter — machine list

Cameras at the sorting table, in three stages

1 · Pilot: record and count

An overhead camera records the table and counts fruit into each bin as a cross-check on the weights.

2 · Research: grade defects

Published work reports YOLO-type models detecting citrus surface defects. We would need our own labelled photos — about 500–2,000 per defect class estimate.

3 · Only at much larger volume: optical grading line

Commercial sorting machines exist; not priced here quote.

Weights are the record. Camera counts are the cross-check.

11Option 1 · The station's rules

The station is a state machine: it cannot be scanned out of order

Command codes on the wall — CMD-START, CMD-FINISH and CMD-RESET printed as QR codes beside the door, so the operator never puts the scanner down to touch a screen.

Rules the station enforces

  • A crate scanned before a van is locked is refused, with a sound: "Scan the van first."
  • A second van scan while loading is refused: "Finish or reset HN897 first."
  • A crate scan takes the scale's latest stable reading from the same station, no older than 5 seconds. No stable reading: "Put the crate on the scale."
  • The same crate twice is refused: "Already on HN897."
  • Weight outside 5–30 kg is refused as implausible placeholder.
  • Every refusal, reset and finish is written to the audit log with one trip ID.

15Stopping loss

How small a theft can a re-weigh catch? About one orange per crate

One medium orange ≈ 131 g (USDA standard medium, 2⅝" across) believed. Juice oranges may be heavier assumed: weigh 20 of yours and we replace the number.
Oranges lose water. Published storage studies report about 0.5–1.3% of weight per day at room temperature, under different conditions believed. For an 18 kg crate that is 4–10 g per hour.
Alarm rule: shortfall > highest expected loss for the hours elapsed + 2 scale steps (2 × 10 g). One missing orange stands out on trips up to about 11 hours; with a 1 g scale, about 13 hours.
Arithmetic on the assumptions above, not a measurement. The pilot weighs control crates twice a day to set the real tolerance.

Your rear-view idea

Crates loaded with numbers facing the back, one camera reading the crate numbers and the plate: it sees only the outer layer of crates. Inner crates are hidden, and glare or a person in front breaks the read without warning.

18Option 2 · Your idea

The employee's phone as the scanner — it works, through the server

  1. Open the staff app. Your TASK-055 plan already has a Flutter Android staff app; this becomes one of its screens.
  2. Scan the station's QR. The phone is paired with scale WS-1 for this session.
  3. Scan a crate. The scale has already sent its reading to the server; the phone shows "18.42 kg · stable" and saves it with one tap.
  4. Scan the van once; every next crate goes on that van. Finish closes the trip.
  5. At the machine: scan the machine's QR sticker (already in the machine registry), the crate and its seal, then re-weigh.

Why through the server, not Bluetooth

Safari on iPhone has no Web Bluetooth believed, and a phone-to-scale link breaks when phones change. Server pairing works on any phone and keeps one record.

Scanning with the camera

A native app reads QR codes from the camera. A web page can too: Chrome on Android has a built-in barcode reader; iPhones need a JavaScript reader (ZXing). Telegram mini apps have their own QR scanner.

No signal in the van

Scans queue on the phone with an idempotency key and sync later, so nothing is counted twice.
Personal phones need a data-protection notice. A scanner gun is faster at a busy door (not yet measured): guns in the warehouse, phones in vans and at machines.

19Scale connection

Connecting the scale: plain HTTPS (REST) — and where MCP fits

Live screenstablet, phone, dashboard

Most affordable scales speak RS232, not REST

A bridge of about AED 150–350 estimate turns any RS232 scale into a network device. Scales that serve REST themselves exist — Mettler-Toledo's IND360 lists a JSON REST API; Arlyn's UpScale indicator too — at industrial prices quote.

Avoid "smart" Bluetooth scales

Most talk only to their own app; no published way for our system to read them. Buy only if the maker publishes an SDK.

Accept only stable readings

The indicator marks a reading stable when it stops moving. The station takes the latest stable reading, rejects motion, and stores the crate's own tare weight.
MCP is for AI assistants, not for devices. It lets an assistant call tools. Placing it between the scale and the record would put a model in the data path — your rule is that models translate and the database computes. Your local build already has a read-only MCP server over Televend data (computed totals, at most 50 rows): the warehouse adds read-only tools there, such as "which van was short today?"

CopilotKit's OpenIntelligentUI and OpenDots: borrow one idea, adopt neither

OpenIntelligentUI

Borrow the idea

What it is: an open-source chat that answers with text, a ready-made component, or interface code the model writes and runs in a sandbox. MIT licence, about 3.9k stars. Next.js front end plus a Python FastAPI agent; OpenAI by default; its router needs a key from a second AI company.

Why not adopt it: a second runtime (Python) on the shared server; company data sent to two more US AI services, against your UAE-hosting decision (D121); and model-written screens can show numbers no query produced.

Borrow: the assistant picks one of our chart components and fills it with the rows the SQL returned. That fits the analytics answer panel.

OpenDots

Not for operations

What it is: a template for long-lived AI agents, each with its own computer (browser, files, shell). MIT licence, about 5.9k stars, marked alpha. Conversations need CopilotKit's Intelligence service; web research uses a third service by default.

Why not: alpha software; agents that act on their own with shell access, where your platform's rule is that no model decides who or whether and nothing is sent without a person's tap; and another outside service to hold company conversations.

An earlier review (D168) already declined Jev, the router OpenIntelligentUI depends on.
