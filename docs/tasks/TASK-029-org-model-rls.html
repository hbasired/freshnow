TASK-029 — The Org Model: Managers, Department Leads, and Who Sees Whom

2026-09-14 · Phase 2 of the task & project portal plan · migration 0009 · every read policy rewritten on one predicate · 12 boundary tests + 4 route tests · CEO edits roles and reporting lines on the People tab

One-sentence summary: The system now knows four kinds of person instead of two — CEO, manager, department lead, employee — and one SQL function decides, for every table, whether a viewer may see somebody's data; the same rule, in TypeScript, decides whether they may give them work.

1 · Why

You chose: managers assign to and see their reports; department leads see their whole department; the CEO sees everything. Before this, resolveRole() was binary and manager_employee_id was an unindexed column nothing enforced. Every read policy said "own row or CEO".

2 · The model

access_roleSeesMay give work to
ceoeveryoneanyone
managertheir direct reports (manager_employee_id = them), in any departmenttheir direct reports
leadtheir direct reports, plus everyone in their departmentthe same set
employeethemselvesthemselves (the bot's Add a task)

access_role is what a person may do; role_title stays the free-text job title they typed at onboarding. Departments compare case-insensitively — Hemanth typed "Production", the seed says "production", and the live data showed the difference the first time the rule ran.

What we have not verified

  • A manager or lead signed in through the browser. The only real accounts are the CEO and Hemanth; the manager and lead behaviours are proven by the route and boundary tests, not by a person clicking as one.
  • Performance of app_can_view_employee at scale. It is STABLE and indexed, and runs once per row per policy; at seven employees it is instant, at hundreds it has not been measured.
  • The bot still sees two roles. resolveRole() is unchanged; a manager in Telegram gets employee behaviour. Manager features in the bot are not in this plan.
  • Department names are free text. Case-insensitive matching handles "Production"/"production"; it does not handle "Prod". A department list is a small later improvement.

Security

  • This phase rewrote live RLS policies. Every branch of the new predicate has a test that a viewer who must not see a row does not, and the pre-existing suite passed unchanged first.
  • app_can_view_employee is SECURITY DEFINER; its search_path is pinned and it reads exactly one table. Execute is granted to freshnow_app only.
  • Role and reporting-line changes are CEO-only and audited with before/after. The CEO row cannot be given another role.
  • Migration 0009 was corrected once (case-insensitive department) after it had been applied to this machine's database only; the function was re-applied in place. It has not shipped anywhere else.
