What was done, and why this way

  • Header: the date, theme, alert, bell, refresh and avatar controls are one ml-auto group, so when they wrap they stay at the right.
  • Panel (D143):
    • measured from the bell, right-aligned under it, clamped 8 px inside both window edges, with its height capped to the space below;
    • rendered into <body>, because the header's backdrop blur makes the header the containing block for any fixed child;
    • closes on Escape.
  • Answered requests (D144): recordConsent marks that person's consent.requested inbox rows read. The worker's sweep settles rows left by people who agreed before this change. No other message is touched.
  • pnpm urls (VF37) asks cloudflared's own metrics server (/quicktunnel, ports 20241–20245) for the name, rather than parsing its log. It prints Phone https://…/app/ with a probe through Cloudflare. It explains the Wi-Fi line's "DOWN" when the API listens on localhost only.
  • Guides: what to restart after a pull (api, worker and bot; not the tunnel); phone prerequisites; getting the address onto the phone; a troubleshooting table for the phone.
