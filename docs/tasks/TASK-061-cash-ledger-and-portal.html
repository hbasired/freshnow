Why it was done this way

Its own tables, the stock ledger's rules

Cash moves like stock — from one place to another, never edited, mistakes reversed — so it follows the same rules, tested the same way. But it is not stock: the warehouse keeper sees all stock and must see no cash, and money is whole fils, never a decimal. Separate tables keep both promises in the database itself, not in a filter someone could forget.

The shift is the unit, as the WhatsApp list was

Kiran's "14/10" list was posted on 15 Oct at 07:12: a night shift's cash belongs to the day it began. A shift holds every entry of one person's working period, adds them up the way the list did (collected − fuel − test − deposited = left), and is what the auditor checks — once, as a whole, instead of message by message.

The driver never sees "should have found"

The driver counts the cash and types the figure. Showing them Televend's figure — before, or after while they may still undo and type again — invites matching it: reporting AED 30 when the box held 35 because Televend missed a sale. That is the blind-count situation, the same reason the stock count hides the ledger. The auditor, the Ops Head, the CEO and Admins see it beside every collection. Some vending software does show drivers the expected amount — where sealed bags are counted in a counting room, not by the driver (D237).

A photo on every entry, because that is today's practice

The group already carries a photo per machine, the fuel receipt and the deposit receipt, and the user decided photos are mandatory for field work. A test note (a 10-dirham note put back into the machine) needs none. Which entries need one is FreshNow's to confirm (A-T61.3).

What was deliberately not built

The reconciliation runs and over/short postings (TASK-062), the bank's or cash-in-transit company's statement, change refills for machines that give change (A-T61.5), reading receipts by OCR (TASK-068), the phone app's offline queue (TASK-063), and the rest of TASK-061 (route plans, visits by QR, fills, part swaps, tickets).

  • Full suite on 19 Oct, typecheck included: 1,108 passed, 5 skipped (the opt-in suites), 0 failed. The run before it failed 2 tests in the bot's consent test, which compared the notice as one message; the longer notice now arrives in two (Telegram's 4,096 characters), so the test now checks every word arrives in order and each part fits.
  • Browser, on the test database with its own API: 35 checks, 0 console errors — a driver's whole shift on a phone (start, collect with a real photo upload, fuel, test note, deposit, undo, end with cash in hand), the auditor's check (Televend "less by AED 5", the photos loading for the auditor, a question), the driver's answer, "all ok"; the keeper refused cash; and the TASK-060 screen fixes (typing 2.5, take back after a refresh, the count kind, figures hidden while counting).
  • Live: 0035 applied 19 Oct (five bookkeeping accounts, the capabilities, six live triggers); the API, worker and bot restarted on the new code; no cash posted to the live ledger by the developer.

What we have not verified

  • A real driver or auditor has not used it. Nobody at FreshNow has recorded a shift's cash or checked one with it yet.
  • Televend's figure on real data. The matching (the collection within 90 minutes, the price, A-T61.4) is tested on made-up rows; it depends on drivers still recording each collection in the Televend Staff App — if they stop, nothing matches.
  • Photos on a real phone's camera: the browser check uploaded a generated JPEG through the real upload route into a store kept in memory; Supabase Storage itself was not used in the check.
  • The receipt fields are typed by hand; nothing reads the receipt (OCR is TASK-068), and nothing checks the deposit against the bank yet (TASK-062).
  • Machines that give change (A-T61.5) and how long cash records are kept (A-T61.6) are assumptions.
  • The notice's new lines are a developer's draft, not FreshNow's or a lawyer's.
  • Speed at a year's volume (thousands of shifts; Televend's figure is computed per collection when a shift is read) — unmeasured.
  • Screen readers, Hindi and Urdu, and working offline.
  • The TASK-060 review's skeptic pass did not run on 18 Oct (usage limit); the independent review of 20 Oct checked those fixes (TASK-060 page) and this task's (below).
  • A Supervisor's blind count is not complete: from the sales list a Supervisor can still count a machine's cash sales since a collection (second pass, cash #2). It waits on FreshNow's choice (D254, A-T61.14).
