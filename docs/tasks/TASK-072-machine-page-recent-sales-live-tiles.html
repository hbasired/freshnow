Why it was done this way

A machine is FreshNow's, not a Televend record

A machine that moves gets a new Televend record (asset 796 has had four, G160). The page follows the machine through its placements, so its history stays with it; the records are listed with their dates.

Televend's stock is an estimate, and says so

Televend's share of capacity left is only as good as the fills logged in the Staff App: it goes below zero when sales outrun them (−0.22 seen live, VF90). Cups left are worked out as that share × each column's capacity (from the newest refill) × the columns, and shown as "about", with the share it came from; a figure below zero is said in words. "Low on stock" is under 25 % (A-T72.1, to confirm).

Fair comparisons only

Today is compared with the same weekday a week before up to the same minute; a day the sync did not read to its end is empty, never zero (the TASK-058 rule).

Expected cash only for cash.read

What each collection should have found is shown only to those who may read it — the CEO, Admin, Ops Head and cash auditor (D237, D254). A Supervisor sees that a collection happened and when.

Not built here

  • Televend's history log of renames and moves (O2.9): its read is not on the allow-list, and was not added in this task. The page shows the moves our placements know.
  • A machine moved within its site (RW.7): the machine's notes are shown; recording a move inside a site is not built.
  • A page listing everything we read from Televend and where it is shown (R10.3): carried to TASK-075.

How it works

machinePage finds the machine by its asset number, gathers the Televend records placed in it (with their dates), and asks Postgres for each part under the viewer's row security: sales by hour and day from tv_vend, refills from tv_refill, collections from tv_event, alarms, visits. Prices: the cash price set on the machine, else AED 10; the card price from the "AED n" in its Televend name, else the cash price (D258). The page computes nothing; it draws what the server sends.

Research findings

  • Televend's stock share in the live mirror (VF90): all 87 enabled records have one; it ranges from −0.22 to 1.00; one record is below zero, eight are under 25 %.
  • Cash at machines named "AED 12" is collected in tens (VF89) — so the name's price is the card price, and cash is priced at the cash price. This task's page priced it so first; Analytics followed in the review (D258).
