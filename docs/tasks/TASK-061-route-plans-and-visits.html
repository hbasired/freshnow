Why it was done this way

The sheet's own words, as data

The tasks and proof columns are the 18 Oct sheet's, word for word ("Kachara remove", "Make free vend", "Check 🚨"), stored as fixed codes so the grid can be computed. A plan is a team's day and shift; a team is whoever is on the plan that day, because the sheet pairs people differently each day (A-T61.10). Removing a stop keeps it, marked removed, because a visit may already point at it.

A sticker link instead of an in-page scanner

A page can read a QR code itself with the Barcode Detection API, but only on HTTPS (MDN) — and this laptop setup is plain HTTP on the local network until the server is deployed (TASK-064). Every Android phone's camera already opens a link printed as a QR code, so the sticker is the link; the number printed on it is the fallback, and the visit records which was used (D242). No location is read.

Missing proof is said, not refused

Refusing to end a visit without the re-sorting photo would leave the visit open and block the driver's next machine; the sheet's practice is a red NO that the Supervisor follows up. So ending is always allowed and answered with what is missing (D243).

No third ledger

The fill and the cash go on the stock and cash ledgers built in TASK-060 and part 1, with their photos and rules; the visit only links to them (D244). The auditor's view of cash does not change, and the driver still never sees Televend's "should have found" (D237).

What was deliberately not built here

  • Part swaps and tickets (part 3), reconciliation and "who did not report" (TASK-062), printed stickers (TASK-065), suggested stops and route optimisation (the 15-point plan).
  • Any ranking or score of drivers from their visits — the grid is about stops, not people (CLAUDE.md).

How it works

Supervisor: plan (team, machines, tasks, crates, proof)→team told once, with the crates→ driver at the machine: sticker link / number→visit: tasks ✓, photos, video, fill, cash→ end: "proof still missing: …"→Proof grid: OK / NO + Televend refill

A visit matches the stop for its machine on the person's current round, unvisited first. The grid takes Televend's refill and Cash Collection within 60 minutes before to 120 minutes after the visit began (A-T61.12). Every write carries an id made in the browser, so a double tap or a retry posts once; every change is audited with its correlation id.

Research findings

  • Barcode Detection API: "available only in secure contexts (HTTPS)", experimental and not Baseline — MDN, BarcodeDetector. Hence the sticker link.
  • Taking a video from a web page on Android: a report from 2025 says Chrome on Android 14 and 15 no longer offers the camera for a plain <input type=file accept="video/*">; adding capture forces a recording through the camera app (and then no file can be picked from the gallery) — addpipe blog. The visit sheet uses capture="environment", so proof is recorded on the spot. Not tried on a real phone (below).
  • Android's 48 dp touch-target guidance (checked 17 Oct for TASK-060) is kept: every button on the visit sheet is at least 48 px.

What we have not verified

  • A real phone: the camera opening a sticker's link (no stickers are printed yet — TASK-065), recording the dispenser video through capture, whether the recording is MP4 and under 15 s (the server refuses longer; the phone does not stop at 15 s), and whether a person who is not signed in lands back on the visit after signing in.
  • A video in the browser check: the dispenser-video path was tested in the API with a stored video record, not by uploading a real recording through the page.
  • Reaching the laptop from a phone: the link works only where the phone can reach the API (same network now; HTTPS after TASK-064).
  • Televend's refill window (−60 / +120 minutes) is a guess until there are real visits to compare (A-T61.12).
  • Crates vs cartons (A-T61.7), which machines always need the video (A-T61.8), when night shifts end (A-T61.9).
  • Live updates to teammates (the doorbell on a plan or visit change) were not watched in a browser this time; the planner's own change now reloads without it.
  • Copy the day before and machines served only by day or night were tested in the API and core, not clicked in the browser.
  • Scale: 60 stops × 6 people per plan and many teams at once were not measured.
  • The notice's wording is a developer's draft (3.0-draft), not FreshNow's or a lawyer's.
  • The independent review ran on 20 Oct (below). Two of its fixes are screen or script only and untested: the proof and tasks shown on visits off the plans, and the demo reset's "[erased]" words.
